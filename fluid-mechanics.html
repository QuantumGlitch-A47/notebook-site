01 / Introduction

Introduction

How fluids push, flow and carry momentum, and how to scale a model test to full size.

The course covers dimensional analysis and similarity with the Buckingham Pi method, control volume analysis for mass and momentum, pressure and hydrostatics including manometers and forces on submerged gates, and continuity and Bernoulli with Pitot probes, venturi meters, tanks, jets and siphons.

02 / Interactive notes

Interactive notes

Key equations with every variable explained, live calculators and worked examples.

2.1Course guide

Four topics: dimensional analysis with a Pi group solver and model test scaling, control volume forces on bends, nozzles and junctions, hydrostatic gates and manometers, and Bernoulli applications. Ends with ten worked examples.
