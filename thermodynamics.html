01 / Introduction

Introduction

How heat turns into work, what limits it, and how gas flows through compressors and nozzles.

The course covers the second law and heat engines, Carnot efficiency and coefficient of performance, entropy and entropy generation, T-s diagrams, ideal gas entropy changes, compressor work, available energy, pure substances, and steady flow with stagnation conditions, choking and propelling nozzles.
