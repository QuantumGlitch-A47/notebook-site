01 / Introduction

Introduction

How signals carry information, and how to read them in both time and frequency.

The course uses Fourier series, Fourier transforms and convolution to move between the time and frequency domains, and applies them to filters and linear systems. It covers analogue and digital modulation and demodulation, bandwidth limits, and simulation of links in noisy channels, with hands-on work on the oscilloscope and spectrum analyser.
