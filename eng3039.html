01 / Introduction

Introduction

How engineering systems move and vibrate, and how to predict it.

The course builds models of mechanical systems, derives their equations of motion and solves them for single and multiple degree of freedom systems, in free and forced motion. A key focus is the response to harmonic inputs, used to judge whether a vibrating structure stays safe.
