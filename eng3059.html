01 / Introduction

Introduction

How well an aircraft performs, and how its design is chosen to get there.

The course covers range with the Breguet equation, climb and descent, and takeoff and landing performance. On the design side it covers choosing the propulsion system, wing planform and airfoil, weight estimation including electric and hybrid aircraft, tail sizing and constraint analysis, all shaped by certification rules, market needs and sustainability.
