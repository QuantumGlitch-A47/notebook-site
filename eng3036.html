01 / Introduction

Introduction

How to build a computer model of an engineering system, and how far to trust its results.

The course covers mathematical modelling and numerical methods for continuous-time simulation, and how the choice of method affects accuracy. It also covers verifying a model, the limits of simulation, and the hardware and software demands of real-time simulation.
