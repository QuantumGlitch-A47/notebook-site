01 / Introduction

Introduction

How aircraft and rockets produce thrust, from the thermodynamics of gas flow to the parts of a gas turbine.

The course covers propellers, axial and centrifugal compressors, turbines, combustion systems, intakes and nozzles, and how each is designed. It compares propulsion methods, looks at pollution from aircraft, and includes lab testing of a gas turbine where compressibility matters.

Noise of a small Cussons jet engine at idle, 30%, 50% and 70% throttle, recorded with a calibrated microphone and analysed with Fourier analysis to link sound level to jet velocity.
